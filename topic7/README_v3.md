# Topic 7, version 3: everything on the real data

Version 3 of the paper is sir's Paper 7 with every experiment run on the real Kaggle table (Medical Appointment No Shows) instead of the simulator, and with our method compared against his methods and against baselines from published work on the same data. It is built only from new files; versions 1 and 2 and all earlier files are unchanged.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `code/run_real.py` | runs sir's whole Paper 7 experiment on the real data (benchmark, learning curves, policies, exploration schedules, no-show shift), plus our structured model, the baselines, data volume and busier clinic studies; writes `code/out/topic7_real/` |
| `code/paper07_v3.py` | builds the paper from `out/topic7_real/results.json` with sir's layout code; every number is computed from that file |
| `code/check_paper_v3.py` | checks the paper: no em dashes, no hyphens beyond names and references, table, figure and reference counts |
| `paper/Paper07_v3_real_data.docx` | version 3 of the paper (same title as Paper 7) |

## Run (from the repo root, with the virtual environment active)

```
python topic7/code/run_real.py          # about 30 minutes; needs topic7/data/KaggleV2-May-2016.csv
python topic7/code/paper07_v3.py        # seconds
python topic7/code/check_paper_v3.py    # must end with ALL CHECKS PASSED
```

`python topic7/code/run_real.py --figures` redraws the figures from the saved results; `--quick` is a two minute smoke test whose output is not committed.

## What the real table cannot give, and what is done instead

1. **No overbooking decisions.** The logs are generated by sir's behaviour policy (his rule with 25% random choices) on the real sessions. Who comes and who does not is what the table records.
2. **No waiting list.** Sir's waiting list rule is kept (1.5 new patients per session, shortened by extra bookings). This is the one simulated part.
3. **No clinics or slots.** A session is all appointments of one neighbourhood on one day (at least 5), with 10 regular slots.
4. **No times within a day.** Overtime and waiting are counted in patients, as in Paper 7.
5. **Only 26 days.** "150 logged days" are 150 runs of 40 sessions drawn from the 506 real sessions of 16 to 25 May; every policy is scored on the 592 sessions of 30 May to 8 June, never used for learning.
6. **No hidden no-show rate.** The state uses the classifier's predicted risk band (slots filled × predicted risk × waiting list), as the problem statement asks.
7. **No shift knob.** The no-show rate of the scoring sessions is moved by +0.08 and -0.05 by flipping recorded outcomes of randomly chosen patients; predictions are not changed. The real drift (21.2% to 18.5%) is reported as well.

## Results on the real scoring sessions (20 replicates)

| Method | Return | Utilisation | Overtime / session | Waiting list / session |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sir: rule | 150.82 | 88.9% | 0.20 | 5.28 |
| Sir: DP | 170.28 ± 0.99 | 90.4% | 0.39 | 2.46 |
| Sir: Monte Carlo | 162.80 ± 2.26 | 89.2% | 0.38 | 2.86 |
| Sir: Q-learning | 167.05 ± 2.11 | 90.1% | 0.42 | 2.53 |
| Sir: oracle DP (10x data) | 173.62 | 91.5% | 0.54 | 1.72 |
| Threshold double booking (published) | 164.53 | 95.5% | 1.02 | 1.76 |
| Fixed booking, k = 2 (baseline) | 174.61 | 91.4% | 0.37 | 2.45 |
| Single session optimiser (published) | 182.03 | 93.8% | 0.51 | 1.83 |
| Ours: Monte Carlo | 169.32 ± 1.70 | 92.4% | 0.65 | 2.00 |
| Ours: Q-learning | 175.16 ± 1.05 | 92.8% | 0.59 | 1.81 |
| **Ours: DP** | **182.54 ± 0.01** | 93.8% | 0.53 | 1.67 |

What changes from the simulator version: on real data sir's learners are well ahead of his rule (+12 to +19, all p < 0.001), because the rule books too few patients for a 19% no-show rate, and the risky direction for the shift is more no-shows, not fewer. What stays: no exploration schedule reaches 90% of the optimum, near greedy Q-learning is the slowest, and the learners stop short of the oracle because rarely logged actions are unsupported. Our structured model is ahead of every method at the nominal rate and at both shifted rates, reaches its score with 25 logged days, and its margin over the single session optimiser grows from 0.2% to 33% as waiting gets costlier.
